# Batch 3 — Flood visuals, access and movement

Completed technical verification on 30 September 2026 in Unity 6000.5.7f1. Scope: B3.1–B3.6 plus the user's requested ocean-style flood graphics. Low-poly geometry and 16:9 are preserved; reference resolution 1920×1080. Human playtest acceptance remains pending.

## Implemented design

- Evacuation work decks, side passages and fitted ramps are prepared at their supported height before departure. They no longer rise under an occupied task. The chosen difficulty determines clearance. Signs and the guide explain the fixed access. Older schema-2 saves migrate to fixed access and return to the unfinished junction, retaining progress; displaced center props are adjusted with their local work area.
- Flood shading shares the active `DynamicSea` ocean's ripple normal map, shallow/deep palette, grazing reflection and highlights. It retains the flood mesh's wet/dry clipping, shallow wave damping, depth-based opacity, wall-safe refraction and currents. Sparse moving surface streaks and a direction/speed readout communicate current. Flood waves stay small.
- Dry walking recovers 1.5 stamina/second; rest recovers 6. Running costs 4/second, moving in wading water costs 0.8/second, swimming costs 2/second (4 when fast), and floating costs 0.25/second. Heavy carrying adds a 1.5/second cost while moving. Ordinary dry walking no longer receives the former exhaustion speed multiplier.
- Hunger increases by 0.05/second, or 0.07 while running. Above 60 it gradually reduces positive stamina recovery, reaching half recovery at 100. Food retains its existing 35-point hunger reduction. Hunger alone does not damage health.
- Underwater breath lasts 40 seconds. After 30 seconds a visible warning and upward swimming assistance help the player surface. After 40 seconds health decreases by 10/second. Surfacing recovers breath at four seconds per second. The bag and guide close with a surface instruction when submerged, so they cannot block swimming while breath runs out. These are game rules, not advice about real-world breath holding.
- At zero health the game pauses in assistance recovery. Resume/Escape cannot bypass it. Assistance returns to clear supported footing, restores 50 health and 60 stamina, records an assisted recovery, and retains completed work and remaining supplies. The player can instead load a saved run. No completion credit is awarded.
- Swimming has separate entry/exit thresholds and a collision-checked step onto a nearby low, clear shore. Vehicle entry is restricted in floodwater and pedestrian task areas, while accompanying residents, or while holding a supply. An obstructed vehicle exit can be recovered through the existing task recovery panel.
- Navigation now separates direction, target distance, footing and current labels, addressing the crowded 720p distance/footing label recorded in Batch 2.

## Flood model scope

SIBUCAO_RAIN uses `groundRelativeInundation`. Its difficulty envelope is an authored scenario: Easy 0.6 m, Normal 0.8 m, Hard 1.2 m, Severe 1.6 m and Extreme 3.5 m peak water depth after 960 storm seconds. These are maximum scenario depths, not measured hazard predictions.

This mode now evaluates the field directly at the scenario time. Local elevation above the lowest adjacent ground reduces a cell's depth multiplier from 1 toward 0.4. Small visual-wave amplitude is reserved within the depth cap. Barrier-aware downhill surface differences produce bounded gameplay current cues. They do not transport volume through a hydraulic solver. Rain, added scenario inflow, drainage and water volume are accounted for explicitly; this bookkeeping is not evidence of physical flood accuracy. Dry refuge exclusion remains active.

The alternate rainfall/runoff mode retains its neighbor-transfer solver. It is experimental, uncalibrated, and now stops catch-up work after a six-millisecond budget (a single step can exceed this budget). It is not the active SIBUCAO_RAIN scenario mode. A visible restore-progress prompt explains a pending catch-up; movement and survival progression wait for the water field.

- [20/20 physical access checks](../Captures/Batch3_20260930/physical-access-checks.txt): actual controller movement up/down the B1, B5 and B9 entry ramps in all five difficulties, plus support along all 18 companion routes at approximately one-metre intervals (6,788 supported samples). ## Acceptance limits

Scripted input and staged screenshots do not establish first-time-player usability or complete continuous-route coverage. Standalone target-hardware performance and XR remain in Batch 7. No flood model calibration or real-world vehicle/survival safety claim is made by this implementation.
