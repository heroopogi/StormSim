# First-time-player acceptance record

Status: **not run**. No participant results are inferred from automated checks.

Use the verified desktop candidate and a participant who has not played StormSim. Record the build identifier, PC/GPU, resolution, role and difficulty. Start a new run using the normal menu and preparation home. Explain only that this is a fictional storm evacuation exercise; let the in-game instructions teach the controls.

Observe whether the participant can collect and pack the bag, leave home, understand the first three-way junction, complete an encounter and continue to the school. Ask them to cancel an interaction, put down and recover an object, save, quit and resume. Include an escort or assistance encounter. Do not count a staged encounter fixture as a completed continuous run.

Record timestamps for the start, leaving home, each encounter, save/quit/resume, reception and handover. Record pauses longer than ten seconds with no progress, the instruction being read, confusion, incorrect expectations, requests for help, and any assistance given. Stop the timer during a researcher interruption and record its duration separately. A participant may stop at any time.

After handover, ask the participant to explain one decision's effect, one unresolved situation, the difference between physical completion and the recorded outcome, and what they would do differently. Compare their explanation with the recorded history and scores. Do not supply the answer before recording their response.

Acceptance requires completing the normal start and first junction without researcher assistance, recovering ordinary mistakes and a saved run without losing progress, reaching school without a progression blocker, and correctly explaining at least one recorded consequence. Record accessibility settings used and check that prompts remain readable in heavy weather.
