# Preparation integrated into SIBUCAO_RAIN

Play Assets/Scenes/SIBUCAO_RAIN.unity, choose Play and a role. Preparation now runs in the actual Northern courtyard home (homes[0], world origin -97, -1.25, 1260), rather than opening the standalone prototype. It retains the real terrain, streets and houses. The previous standalone scene remains available as a prototype, but is not the entry point for this integration.

Normal flow: clear-weather home opening, TV bulletin, search household storage, optionally gather supplies on the dining table, retrieve/open the bag and pack before sleep, visit neighbors and receive warning confirmations, return home, review the bed summary and confirm sleep. The sleep fade restarts the original wake-up sequence in the same home, including eyelids, TV bulletin/glitches and standing up. Both clocks start at zero; the existing storm timeline then proceeds normally. Going outside to visit neighbors does not start the storm. An official evacuation instruction still allows early departure.

22 added household activities: four pre-cut window panels; four accessible fasteners/restraints; four dry-floor cleanup areas; two surface drain grates; bagging refuse; clearing the exit; three sheltered-storage tasks; ground-level roof-defect reporting; confirming an emergency contact; checking high document storage. Tasks require the matching carried tool/material and holding E or the right trigger while facing the target. Installed panels/fittings and removed debris change visibly. Reporting roof damage does not count as structural repair. Major roof/structural work is not simulated as a quick player repair.

The three other existing furnished homes are accessible through their front doors. Each contains one seated resident and one resident sweeping. Use E to talk after checking the TV: share the warning, ask about needs, then confirm a meeting plan. Six separate acknowledgements are saved. Idle clips reuse existing humanoid assets; seated and sweeping movements are procedural approximations, not new motion capture. The houses retain their existing locations along the map, so walking to all three takes time.

Controls: WASD/mouse move and look; Shift run (6.5 m/s); Space jump; F pick up/drop a supply or retrieve the bag; B open/close the carried bag; P animate the held supply into the open bag; Q put the bag down; G unpack; E interact or hold to work; Tab checklist; F5/F9 save/load. The bag occupies the right hand and supplies use the free hand. In VR, hold the bag with the right grip, toggle its opening with the right trigger, then use the left grip to grab a supply and release it over the highlighted opening. Left trigger interacts while carrying the bag. Headset interaction and comfort still require hands-on validation.

Packing sequence: check the television, open the closet/chest and find supplies on household surfaces. Gather supplies on the table if desired. Retrieve the bag, open it, then grab one supply with the free hand and insert it. The flexible zippered opening, sliding zipper pull, insertion motion, item confirmation and miniature packed-supply previews provide feedback. G retrieves an item. Bulk household water belongs in the household supply area. The bag has an 8 kg scenario limit.

Visit residents and press E to share the warning. Their first acknowledgment records the contact immediately; repeat conversations do not increase the count again. The HUD/checklist show confirmed contacts and people reached (four fictional residents per family placeholder group, one per original individual resident). Further interactions discuss needs and a meeting plan. Warn as many contacts as desired, then return home. The bed's first interaction summarizes warnings and packed items; its second confirms sleep. A carried bag closes and is staged indoors before the fade. The former mandatory next-morning packing stage is removed from this flow.

The new spawn-neighborhood pass adds representative furnished interiors and fictional four-person placeholder families to mapped footprints within 65 metres of the first home. The existing exteriors are retained, with door openings cut into separate copies of their combined meshes. These households are not surveyed demographics. Family models are simple replaceable placeholders; final character animation remains future work.
