## One continuous preparation run

- [ ] Understand and choose Guided or Explore without explanation.
- [ ] Read the warning, start preparation, and replay it from the guide.
- [ ] Find the bag, open storage, pick up a supply, check it, and pack it.
- [ ] Repeat with another supply without outside coaching.
- [ ] Switch coaching off and on; confirm progress and supplies stay intact.
- [ ] Distinguish home water reserves from the small travel bottle.
- [ ] Use household help at the water shelf; explain the household quantity target.
- [ ] Fit batteries and test equipment; inspect/close first aid; seal documents.
- [ ] Charge a device; explain partial charge, the practice target, and why evacuation must not wait for charging.
- [ ] Open the bag, read its weight and item readiness, and retrieve a chosen item.
- [ ] Recover a dropped or incorrectly placed item without restarting.
- [ ] Attempt an unsuitable placement and understand the feedback.
- [ ] Try press-once household work; interrupt and resume it.
- [ ] Notice a confirmation before undoing a protective action.
- [ ] Explain the four household members' responsibilities and where to reunite.
- [ ] Explain the primary route and when a marked side passage is appropriate.
- [ ] If visiting a neighbor, hear a need and discuss the meeting plan; do not treat acknowledgement as proof of shelter arrival.
- [ ] Explain why optional outdoor/elevated work stops as conditions worsen.
- [ ] Review supplies before leaving or resting; understand that incomplete chores do not block departure.
- [ ] Save, resume, and confirm bag contents, held item, charge, and partial work.
- [ ] Leave and continue into evacuation with the expected bag and supplies.
- [ ] At school, review preparation and any observed consequences; distinguish preparation from guaranteed protection.

## Additional short checks

- [ ] Explore from a fresh start: objective and small action hints are enough to begin.
- [ ] Rest branch: morning advisory arrives, supplies remain, and leaving works.
- [ ] Evacuation order: departure works immediately without a forced long review.
- [ ] Read at 1920×1080 and 1280×720 with 130% text; scroll the bag and guide.
- [ ] Remap a control and confirm prompts match it.
- [ ] If testing VR, validate hand controls, guide readability, and safe ladder descent on the actual headset.
