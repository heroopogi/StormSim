DRRMO AND ADVISER GAMEPLAY IMPROVEMENT CHECKLIST
================================================

Scope: Requested improvements to preparation, the go-bag, difficulty
progression, and flood-safety learning in SIBUCAO_RAIN.

Legend:
[x] Present in the current source
[ ] Proposed, incomplete, or awaiting confirmation

The 16 October 2026 update has Unity compile errors. A feature found in the
source is not considered runtime-verified until the project compiles and is
checked in Play Mode.


CURRENT PREPARATION AND ROLE FLOW
---------------------------------

[x] Preparation includes a weather bulletin, household activities, go-bag
    packing, optional neighbor contact, and a departure or rest choice.

[x] The current menu starts the player as Resident without offering a role
    choice; difficulty selection remains.

[x] Players can choose Guided or Explore preparation. Guidance can be changed
    later.

[x] Player role selection is bypassed in the current menu flow. The legacy
    role field remains for compatibility.

[x] The update adds a one-day-before opening, voice and subtitle guidance,
    a physical handbook with a progress pencil, and an item-information panel.

[ ] Replace the prescriptive "What to Prepare" list with a short current
    objective, the active warning, and contextual item guidance.

[x] Optional preparation can be left unfinished. An evacuation instruction
    takes priority over household tasks.


GO-BAG AND ITEM INTERACTIONS
----------------------------

[x] The desktop bag panel shows packed-item names, weight, readiness, bag
    capacity, and individual retrieval actions.

[x] The departure review summarizes packed supplies and the household plan.

[x] Retrieving an item restores it as a physical object in the player's hand.
    The player can then drop it manually.

[x] The storm inventory has separate "Take out" and "Drop selected" actions.

[ ] Add useful inventory grouping or sorting, such as Communication, Health,
    Food and Water, and Documents.

[ ] Confirm that the new preparation item-information panel has a valid
    purpose description for every relevant supply.

[ ] Change the preparation bag's removal action to place the item directly in
    a safe, reachable place in the world, with no loss or duplication.

[ ] Show a brief pickup explanation and connect representative supplies to an
    in-game check or action where appropriate.


FLOOD DIFFICULTY AND TASK PROGRESSION
-------------------------------------

[x] The storm has five authored difficulty profiles, and floodwater rises over
    the scenario timeline.

[ ] Make water-height changes and their effects on routes clear at relevant
    mission stages.

[ ] Define how mission stage and selected difficulty affect the water profile
    and route availability.

[ ] Keep optional chores from causing sudden water increases. Evacuation
    remains the priority as conditions worsen.

[ ] Keep flood depth and route changes tied to the authored game scenario. Do
    not present scenario values as calibrated local flood predictions.


APPLY THE DRRMO FLOOD GUIDANCE
------------------------------

[ ] Use the leaflet's Monitoring, Alert, and Evacuate states to drive
    consistent radio, television, and objective updates.

[x] An evacuation instruction already overrides unfinished preparation and
    optional activities.

[ ] Provide a safe route to higher ground when flooding blocks a route. Do not
    reward walking or driving through floodwater.

[ ] If an aftermath activity is included, require an official all-clear before
    return and inspection. Teach players to avoid and report hazards such as
    downed electrical wires.

[ ] Confirm local wording, warning stages, routes, and emergency information
    with DRRMO before presenting them as official guidance.


DECISIONS TO CONFIRM BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION
------------------------------------------

[ ] Confirm that "remove roles" means removing the player role-selection
    screen. The current source already bypasses this screen.

[ ] Confirm that "remove what to prepare" means removing the prescriptive
    checklist from the UI while retaining preparation gameplay and contextual
    item explanations.

[ ] Confirm that "flood height per task" means staged progression through
    required mission phases, rather than raising water after every optional
    task.


ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
-------------------

[ ] The bag clearly explains what the player packed, why relevant items matter,
    and how to retrieve or drop them.

[ ] The opening flow no longer asks the player to choose a role.

[ ] The preparation UI gives useful next steps without relying on the removed
    prescriptive list.

[ ] Flood progression is visible, follows the selected difficulty, and
    supports evacuation decisions.

[ ] DRRMO lessons appear in player choices and consequences across preparation
    and flooding.

[ ] A first-time participant completes a continuous playthrough and can explain
    the warnings, important supplies, evacuation decision, and flood hazards.
