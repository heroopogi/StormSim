# Stalled cars: four starting locations

B2 now carries a station-local galvanized roadside enclosure. Continuous solid panels close the immediate roadside gaps, with taller car-side returns to prevent climbing off the hood onto the shoulder. The entrance and far road remain open; this is a local encounter enclosure, not a global barrier across the entire open-world map. Backtracking remains possible. Existing missed-task handling is unchanged.

All four authored home layouts bind all four physical choices inside the car passage. C0's alley traversal is replaced by clearing the wedged debris and walking between the cars; C3's swimming route now stays between the cars rather than heading out onto the roadside. C1 retains squeeze input and C2 retains the hood climb. C3 still requires swimming and its existing water-tier eligibility. Car meshes and their chassis/cabin colliders move together to leave a central body-sized gap.

The four alternative encounters share a front work area: only the selected choice's debris is visible/solid, with C0 visible before selection. This avoids putting four overlapping sets of debris in the car gap or putting movable boards inside the chassis. Every choice retains its existing active-work duration: C0 80 seconds, C1 65, C2 60, C3 70. The shared FinishPhysicalChoice handoff is unchanged.

Only C0's label and success description change in DecisionChoice assets. A before/after serialized comparison, excluding those two presentation strings, verified all other choice data unchanged. No schema, probability, score cap, health/stamina, flag, consequence, inventory or D-task changes. The shorter C0 traversal removes the former 118-metre alley cost; this is a balance change even though the authored score values are preserved and should be reviewed with the existing score-cap discussion.

Authoring: `SibucaoCarEnclosureBuilder.Install()` rebuilds B2 geometry and its four home bindings in Edit mode. `Validate()` checks every variant stays in the enclosure, continuous side-panel collision, and central capsule clearance. The four-home Play-mode fixture also checks all 12 movable pieces of each choice for physical clearance. This is not a complete human playthrough of every choice and water tier.

The production-input B2 C0 fixture passed with 12 moved/released pieces, 80.00006 credited active seconds and approximately 85 seconds wall time. Walking from before the first waypoint through the central passage then produced exactly one resolution record. Fixture teleports between work groups are test setup, not gameplay. The test exposed and fixed road-height placement on slopes and unintended E-driven switching into the nearby squeeze choice; an explicit switch attempt while working was rejected without losing progress. Cancelling first still permits choosing another route.
